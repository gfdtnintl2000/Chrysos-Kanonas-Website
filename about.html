Rooted in the Golden Rule, focused on practical enterprise growth.

Who We Are

Chrysos Kanonas Company Limited is a private company incorporated in Kenya. The name “Chrysos Kanonas” comes from the Greek expression for “The Golden Rule,” meaning doing to others as we would like them to do to us.

Our company supports small-scale entrepreneurs and agribusiness clients by supplying modern tools, machinery and technology-aligned solutions.
